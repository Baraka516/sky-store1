About Us

SkyStore is a modern electronics store in Rwanda, providing high-quality digital devices at affordable prices.

We started in 2024 with the goal of making premium technology accessible to everyone in Rwanda.

Our mission is to deliver fast, reliable, and quality service to our customers.

Mission

To help people access quality electronics easily.

Vision

To become the leading electronics store in Rwanda.

Values

Integrity, Quality, Trust and Excellence.
